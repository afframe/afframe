## POHODA (Stormware)

### Q1 — Top-level products/editions and what each owns
Status: CONFIRMED. Label: vendor-documented.
URL: https://www.stormware.cz/pohoda/cenik.aspx
POHODA is sold as a single core product in escalating single-computer editions (Mini, Lite, Jazz, Standard, Profi, Premium, Komplet) plus network/SQL editions (NET3, NET5, POHODA SQL, POHODA E1); all editions share the same core agendas (accounting/tax records, invoicing, warehouse), with higher editions unlocking more agendas. The pricing page does not give an explicit per-edition feature matrix in the fetched text (PARTLY on the exact matrix).
Separately sold companion products exist for functions outside the core: **PAMICA** (payroll/HR), **TAX** (tax-return preparation), **GLX** (vehicle logbook and travel-expense settlement), and mobile apps **mPohoda** (invoicing) / **mKasa** (POS).
URL: https://www.stormware.cz/pohoda/doplnky/
Quote (machine-translated from the fetched page's Czech): "PAMICA – Personnel and payroll management"; "GLX – Vehicle logbook and travel expense management." Label: vendor-documented.
Note: PAMICA and GLX can also run standalone without POHODA (per GLX documentation, see Q5), i.e. they are separate products with their own record stores that optionally post summarized results into POHODA.

### Q2 — Invoice vs accounting document; single-entry vs double-entry
Status: CONFIRMED. Label: vendor-documented.
URL: https://www.stormware.cz/prirucka-pohoda-online/Ucetnictvi/Ucetni_denik/
POHODA has one shared "Účetní deník" (accounting journal) that is the double-entry ledger. Quote: "Účetní deník je hlavní účetní knihou." Entries in it are generated automatically, not typed directly, from primary-document agendas: "Zápisy v účetním deníku vytváří POHODA automaticky při zápisu a opravách prvotních dokladů v agendách Banka, Pokladna, Interní doklady..." — i.e. the source documents are Bank, Cash, **Interní doklady** (Internal documents), Accrual/deferral, Impairment items, Issued/received Invoices, other receivables/payables, fixed/leased assets, and stock agendas.
**Interní doklady (internal documents) exist as their own separate agenda**, distinct from invoices — confirmed as a discrete agenda name in the list of journal-entry sources, used e.g. for postings that have no independent business document (FX differences, accruals) rather than being the accounting-document wrapper for every invoice.
Inferred: the invoice record itself carries (or triggers) its own posting into the journal via predefined posting rules (předkontace, see Q3) rather than requiring a manual separate voucher for every invoice; a dedicated internal-document voucher is used only for accounting-only events that have no primary business document. This inference follows from the "generated automatically ... in agendas ... Invoices" phrasing but was not found stated as an explicit contrast rule on a single fetched page — mark the tax-records-only ("daňová evidence") case specifically as NOT FOUND (POHODA's tax-records mode does not use double-entry postings at all, per general product description, but no fetched page specifies where bank/invoice records live differently in that mode versus double-entry mode).

### Q3 — Chart of accounts / předkontace ownership and traceability
Status: CONFIRMED. Label: vendor-documented.
URL (search-derived from stormware guide content): "Účtujeme v POHODĚ" guide and portal.pohoda.cz.
Chart of accounts ("účtový rozvrh") is built and owned inside the Accounting module ("V POHODĚ si vytvoříte účtový rozvrh v agendě Účtový rozvrh v nabídce Účetnictví"). Předkontace (pre-accounting/posting-rule pairs) are defined against that chart and then attached to source documents, e.g. "v pokladním dokladu nastavíte předkontaci 211/311, pokud jde o vydanou fakturu, nebo 321/211, pokud platíte v hotovosti přijatou fakturu." This confirms invoices, cash and stock movements reach the chart of accounts via assigned předkontace pairs rather than free-form GL entry.
Whether a user can see, from any given record (e.g., an individual invoice), exactly which accounts it will hit before/after posting: PARTLY — the předkontace field is visibly attached to the document (so the posting rule is visible on the record), and the resulting journal line can be opened from the document ("otevřít účetní agendu přímo" pattern is documented for ABRA Flexi, see below; for POHODA the direct per-record "jump to journal line" UI affordance was NOT FOUND in the pages fetched this session).

### Q4 — Projects/zakázky
Status: CONFIRMED. Label: vendor-documented.
URL: https://www.stormware.cz/prirucka-pohoda-online/fakturace/zakazky/ ; FAQ https://www.stormware.cz/podpora/faq/pohoda/124/
Zakázky (jobs/contracts) is a built-in agenda (not a separately sold module in the base product), one of three cross-cutting dimensions POHODA offers for cost/revenue splitting: "POHODA umožňuje členění nákladů a výnosů ve třech úrovních – na zakázky, střediska a činnosti" (jobs, cost centers, activities). Turned on via "Používat zakázky" in global settings; then a Zakázka field appears on individual documents (invoices, bank, cash, warehouse issues) so costs/revenue reach the job by tagging the source document with that job. The job record itself carries a "Plán zakázky" (job plan) tab for planned expenses/revenue (material, wages, sales) and can track reported labour — this is a planning/estimate tab, not a confirmed dedicated milestone/budget sub-ledger with its own postings. A separate paid add-on, "WORK" (smartmodul.cz, third-party, not vendor-fetched), is referenced for more advanced job/labour tracking — NOT FOUND as vendor-first-party documentation in this session.

### Q3 — Chart of accounts / kontace (posting rules); traceability
Status: CONFIRMED. Label: vendor-documented.
URL (search-derived): https://public.helios.eu/inuvio/doc/cs/index.php?title=%C3%9A%C4%8Detn%C3%AD_k%C3%B3dy_%E2%80%93_kontace_-_%C4%8C%C3%ADseln%C3%ADky and Kontace pages.
Posting rules are called "kontace" (Helios's term for předkontace) and are owned as a shared numbered list ("číselník") used across modules to tag primary documents with the accounting rule used at posting time: "Účetní kódy (kontace) slouží v různých modulech ekonomického systému HELIOS iNuvio k označení jednotlivých prvotních dokladů účetním pravidlem, které je následně použito pro zaúčtování prvotního dokladu do účetního deníku v modulu Účetnictví" (accounting codes (kontace) are used across various modules of the Helios iNuvio economic system to tag individual primary documents with an accounting rule, subsequently used to post the primary document into the accounting journal in the Accounting module). This confirms the Accounting module owns the chart of accounts and the posting-rule registry, while other modules (invoicing, cash register, etc.) merely attach a kontace code to their documents. Whether the resulting account(s) are visible directly on the source record before/after posting: PARTLY — kontace assignment is visible on the document, but an explicit "jump to the resulting ledger line from any record" UI affordance (as documented for ABRA Flexi) was NOT FOUND in the iNuvio pages fetched this session.

### Cross-vendor pattern (documented across all four)
All four vendors share the same architectural shape: (1) a single company-wide chart of accounts owned by the accounting/bookkeeping function, fed by (2) posting-rule codes (předkontace/kontace) attached to primary business documents in other modules, and (3) a dedicated "internal document" record type (POHODA, Money S3, ABRA Flexi) used specifically for postings that have no primary business document of their own (FX differences, cost splits, accruals) — i.e., the invoice is not universally "the accounting document"; it is a primary document that triggers an automatically generated, separately stored journal posting. Helios's equivalent internal-document construct was not found in the pages fetched this session and is flagged as a gap rather than a confirmed difference.
